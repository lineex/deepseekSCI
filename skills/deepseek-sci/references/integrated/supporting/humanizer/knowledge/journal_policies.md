# Journal AI Policies - Detailed Comparison

## JAMA Network Guidelines

### Disclosure Requirements
- **Location:** Abstract, Methods section, AND Acknowledgments
- **Required Content:**
  - Software name and version
  - Manufacturer/developer
  - Specific tasks performed
  - Statement of author responsibility

### Sample Disclosure Template
```
"[Author initials] used [Tool Name] (version X.X, [Manufacturer]) 
to assist with [specific task, e.g., language editing, literature summarization]. 
All AI-generated content was reviewed, verified, and revised by [Author names], 
who take full responsibility for the accuracy and integrity of the final manuscript."
```

### Prohibited Uses
- AI listed as author (violates ICMJE authorship criteria)
- Unverified AI-generated references
- AI-created data or fabricated results
- Undisclosed AI assistance in content generation

## NEJM Guidelines

### Core Principles
1. **Transparency:** All AI assistance must be disclosed
2. **Accountability:** Human authors remain fully responsible
3. **Verification:** All AI output must be independently verified

### Disclosure Location
- Methods section (for research methodology AI use)
- Acknowledgments (for writing assistance)

### Special Considerations
- Copyright implications for AI-generated content
- Patient privacy concerns with AI data processing
- Research ethics committee awareness

---

## Critical Care Medicine (CCM) / SCCM

### Augmented vs Artificial Intelligence Distinction
SCCM emphasizes the difference between:
- **Acceptable:** Electronic tools for data analysis, literature search, statistical computation
- **Discouraged:** AI replacing human critical thinking in manuscript drafting

### Policy Highlights
- Strong discouragement of AI-generated original text
- Required disclosure of any AI involvement
- Emphasis on demonstrable human intellectual contribution
- Hiding AI role = academic misconduct

### Recommended Approach
"Use AI as a sophisticated calculator, not as a ghost writer"

---

## ATS Journals

### Disclosure Requirements
- Title page footnote indicating AI use
- Methods section description
- Tool identification and purpose

### Unique Concerns
- Protection of peer review confidentiality
- Prohibition on submitting manuscripts to AI tools during review
- Concerns about proprietary information exposure

## ICMJE Authorship Criteria (Referenced by All Journals)

For authorship credit, individuals must meet ALL FOUR criteria:

1. **Conception/Design OR Data Acquisition/Analysis**
2. **Drafting OR Critical Revision**
3. **Final Approval of Published Version**
4. **Accountability for All Aspects**

> [!IMPORTANT]
> AI systems cannot meet criteria 3 and 4, making AI authorship fundamentally impossible under current standards.
